# PRIVOX PTT — Radio (Inrico T320). User Guide

Current for **PRIVOX PTT T320 1.6** (`versionCode 7`).

This radio runs on the **PRIVOX PTT** system: it talks over the internet (WiFi or
SIM), just like a walkie‑talkie — press the button to talk, release to listen.
On a channel (group) everyone in that group hears you.

---

## 1. What's on the radio

- **Screen** (touch) — shows groups and members.
- **Orange side button (PTT)** — push‑to‑talk. The main button.
- **D‑pad with arrows** (up/down/left/right) and a **center button** — on‑screen
  navigation.
- **Back button** (curved arrow) — step back / minimize.
- **Side knob** — volume.
- **Red button** — power / end.

---

## 2. Power on and network

1. **Power on:** hold the power button until the boot logo appears.
2. **Connect to the internet:**
   - **WiFi:** Settings → Wi‑Fi → pick a network → enter the password.
   - or a **SIM card** with mobile data (if installed).
3. Open the **PRIVOX PTT** app (icon on the screen).

---

## 3. Sign in (first time)

On first launch the radio asks for your **login and password** — enter the
credentials you were given and tap **Sign in**.

After that the session is **remembered**: on later power‑ons you don't need to sign
in again — the radio opens straight to the main screen. It only asks again if you
sign out yourself (see section 8).

---

## 4. Main screen — group list

After signing in you see the **list of groups (channels)**. Each group shows how
many members are currently online.

Navigate with the **D‑pad**:

| Action | Button |
|---|---|
| Move through the list | **arrows ↑ / ↓** |
| Enter a group (open its members) | **center button** |
| Go back (to the group list) | **Back button** (curved arrow) or **arrow ←** |

You can also use the **touchscreen** with your finger — whichever is easier.

---

## 5. How to talk (transmit)

1. Pick the **group** you want (enter it with the center button — it becomes the
   active group).
2. **Press and hold the orange side button (PTT)** and speak.
   The screen turns **green — TRANSMITTING**.
3. **Release** the button to listen for replies.

> PTT always talks to your **active group** — the one you have entered.

Incoming audio plays automatically through the loud speaker.

---

## 6. Group members

After entering a group (center button) you see the **member list**:

- **green dot and green phone** — member is **online**;
- **blue dot and blue phone** — the Android phone is asleep but can be woken by
  a call;
- **gray dot and gray phone** — member is currently unavailable;
- whoever is talking is marked with an icon.

---

## 7. Private call to a member

You can call an online member or wake a sleeping Android phone:

1. Enter a group and find a member with a green or blue phone icon.
2. Highlight them with the arrows and press the **center button** (or tap the phone
   icon next to the name).
3. The member receives a **call**. A blue Android phone is woken through Firebase.

---

## 8. Volume, SOS, sign out

- **Volume:** turn the **side knob**.
- **SOS:** the alert icon on the screen — sends an emergency signal.
- **Sign out (switch user):** the sign‑out icon in the top‑right corner. It asks
  for confirmation **"Log out?"** — choose **Log out** only if you really want to
  sign out (you'll need to sign in again afterwards).

> To just put the radio aside — **don't sign out**. The Back button (minimizes the
> app) or the power button is enough; your session is kept.

---

## 9. Messages and photos

Open **CHAT**, then select a group or user.

- the paperclip opens existing photos and documents;
- the camera icon takes a new photo with the T320 built-in camera;
- the maximum file size is 10 MB.

Allow camera access when version 1.6 asks for it on first launch.

---

## 10. Troubleshooting

- **Too quiet (in or out)** — check the volume knob.
- **Won't sign in / asks every time** — check the internet (WiFi/SIM) and that the
  login is correct; contact your administrator.
- **No groups / empty** — make sure you have internet; your groups are assigned by
  the administrator.
- **App seems frozen** — close and reopen it (Back button → open the PRIVOX PTT
  icon again).
